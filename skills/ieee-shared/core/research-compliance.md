# IEEE engineering research-compliance routing

This is a readiness screen, not legal, ethics-board, spectrum-regulatory, export-control, or cybersecurity advice. For every item use `required`, `not applicable`, or `AUTHOR_INPUT_NEEDED`; never infer approval, consent, permission, license, exemption, or authorization.

## Applicability gates

- Human participants, user studies, teleoperation logs, biometrics, or identifiable imagery: verify ethics/IRB status where applicable, consent, privacy, demographic definitions, exclusion criteria, and publication permission.
- Human–robot interaction or autonomous systems: state safety envelope, supervision, emergency stop, incident handling, operating constraints, and whether public deployment was authorized.
- Public-road, airspace, maritime, medical, critical-infrastructure, or industrial deployment: verify the applicable institutional and regulatory permissions from author evidence.
- Communications experiments: document lawful spectrum use, transmit-power and location constraints, licensed/test-band status, interference containment, and operator authorization where relevant.
- Cybersecurity, adversarial, jamming, malware, or penetration experiments: verify controlled scope, authorization, harm containment, responsible disclosure, and redaction of operationally dangerous details.
- Third-party datasets, network traces, maps, code, firmware, and pretrained models: verify license, privacy terms, redistribution rights, version, provenance, and train/test leakage controls.
- Synthetic or generated data: disclose generation process, model/tool version, validation, limitations, and whether generated content enters training, evaluation, or only illustration.
- Dual-use or safety-critical claims: bound demonstrated capability, state foreseeable misuse and limitations, and avoid operational instructions that create disproportionate risk.
