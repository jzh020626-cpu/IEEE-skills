# Benchmark lens

- Audit construction pipeline, inclusion/exclusion rules, annotation or ground truth, splits, versioning, licenses, and leakage controls.
- Separate scale from coverage, validity, difficulty, reproducibility, and practical usefulness.
- Inspect metric choice, aggregation, uncertainty, missing-data policy, baseline implementation, compute budgets, and ranking stability.
- For network/testbed benchmarks, record topology, traffic/channel models, emulation fidelity, protocol settings, and repeatability.
- Require evidence that the benchmark reveals meaningful capability gaps rather than merely producing a leaderboard.
