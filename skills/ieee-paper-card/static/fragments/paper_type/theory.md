# Theory lens

- Enumerate definitions, assumptions, lemmas, theorems, proof dependencies, guarantees, and boundary conditions.
- Check whether stability, convergence, feasibility, optimality, regret, capacity, or complexity claims match the formal statement.
- Separate sufficient from necessary conditions and asymptotic from finite-sample or finite-time claims.
- Inspect counterexamples, conservatism, parameter knowledge, observability/controllability, communication assumptions, and computational realizability.
- Map theoretical claims to numerical or physical validation without treating simulation as proof.
