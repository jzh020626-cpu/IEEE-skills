# IEEE Results Section

Results are the shortest sufficient evidence chain that establishes and bounds
the paper's claims. Load `../../../../ieee-shared/core/main-text-discipline.md`
before deciding what belongs in the main text.

Classify each result as core evidence, necessary support, qualification,
robustness, scenario/model heterogeneity, provenance/configuration detail,
alternative inference, or an edge/failure case. Keep conclusion-changing
boundaries visible; move non-central audit detail to Supplementary Material or
the artifact record with stable pointers.

For robotics, control, and communications, organize around decisive claims,
aligned baselines, ablations or mechanism tests, uncertainty, robustness,
latency/overhead, scalability, safety or feasibility, and failure boundaries as
applicable. Do not narrate experiments chronologically or treat every completed
run as main-text evidence.

End each evidence unit with the bounded inference it supports. Local
interpretation is acceptable only when the evidence is visible and the wording
does not outrun the tested platform, channel, topology, task, or regime.
