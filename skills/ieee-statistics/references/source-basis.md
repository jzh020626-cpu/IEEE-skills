# Source basis

Use sources in this order:

1. User-supplied manuscript, raw/summary data, protocol, code, analysis plan, reviewer comments, and target-journal instructions.
2. The named IEEE publication's current Information for Authors and any field-specific reproducibility or artifact policy.
3. Authoritative statistical standards or primary methodological references applicable to the design.
4. Conservative engineering reporting practice.

Do not claim that IEEE mandates one universal statistical test. Require enough detail to identify the independent unit, repetitions, metric, aggregation, model/test, assumptions, uncertainty, multiplicity policy, software, and exclusions.
