# IEEE Peer-Review Criteria

Current official IEEE Author Center guidance is the default general source. The named journal's reviewer form and Information for Authors override generic wording.

## Evaluation axes

IEEE's peer-review guidance asks whether the article:

- fits the publication's scope;
- contains original material distinct from prior publications;
- makes a significant contribution or advancement;
- uses sound methods and experimental design;
- reports, analyzes, and interprets data correctly;
- presents results clearly and reproducibly;
- reaches conclusions supported by results;
- cites relevant, sufficient, balanced literature;
- complies with ethical and journal requirements.

## Reviewer conduct

- Provide a prompt, thorough, impartial, constructive review.
- Use professional tone and specific, actionable reasoning.
- Protect confidentiality and do not contact authors directly.
- Recuse for actual, perceived, or potential conflicts.
- Do not request irrelevant/excessive references or use citation coercion.
- Alert the editor to suspected ethics/integrity issues without presenting suspicion as proven misconduct.
- The editor synthesizes reviews and makes the final decision.

## AI and confidential manuscripts

Official IEEE reviewer guidance warns reviewers not to use external generative-AI tools on confidential submissions. This local skill is appropriate for author-owned pre-submission self-review or material the user is authorized to process. If the input is an actual confidential manuscript assigned for peer review, stop and follow the journal's confidentiality/AI policy.
