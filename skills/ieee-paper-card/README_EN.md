# `ieee-paper-card`

Deep-read one robotics, control, communications, networking, industrial-informatics, or adjacent engineering paper and produce a source-grounded Sections 01–16 Paper Card. The workflow audits problem–method–evidence logic, equations and assumptions, experimental boundaries, critical analysis, and testable research hypotheses.

Inputs may be a PDF, DOI, arXiv or publisher page, pasted text, or an `ieee-reader` source map. Select an `algorithm`, `system`, `theory`, `benchmark`, `application`, or `review` lens. Missing evidence must be marked `Not assessable`.

Use `ieee-reader` for a bilingual full-text artifact, `ieee-reviewer` for formal peer review, and `ieee-academic-search` for externally verified novelty or field-history checks.
