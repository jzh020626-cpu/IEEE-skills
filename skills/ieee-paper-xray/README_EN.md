# `ieee-paper-xray` Skill

[中文说明](README.md)

Explain an engineering paper through its method logic, worked mathematics and evidence boundaries.

## What To Use It For

Explain an engineering paper through its method logic, worked mathematics and evidence boundaries.

## Typical Requests

Explain why this control paper uses this design and work through its key equations.

## What You Need To Provide

The paper and available appendices; optionally the section, equation and reading language.

## Outputs

Source-anchored explanations, labelled teaching examples, assumptions and evidence gaps.

## Boundaries

Does not replace a bilingual reader, a structured 01–16 Paper Card or independent peer review.

## Related Skills

ieee-reader, ieee-paper-card, ieee-reviewer
