# Note to Practitioners

Use only for T-ASE or another venue that explicitly requires an equivalent section.

- Keep it separate from the Abstract and place it according to the current journal instructions.
- Explain the practical problem, how the proposed result may be used, the conditions/assumptions needed, present limitations, and realistic deployment maturity.
- Avoid equations, citation lists, novelty slogans, and a second abstract-style methods/results recap.
- Do not promise deployment, safety, productivity, robustness, or cost benefits beyond supplied evidence.
- Verify any word limit and placement against the current T-ASE author page before submission.
