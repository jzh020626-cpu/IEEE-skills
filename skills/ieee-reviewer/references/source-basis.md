# Source Basis

Use sources in this order:

1. The manuscript and evidence supplied by the user.
2. The target journal's current reviewer form and Information for Authors.
3. `ieee-peer-review-criteria.md` and its official IEEE Author Center sources.
4. `domain-specific-review-gates.md` as claim-dependent technical prompts.

The review must distinguish:

- `supported by supplied material`;
- `weak or internally inconsistent`;
- `not assessable from supplied material`;
- `requires current journal-rule verification`.

Three reviewer reports are a local self-review format, not an IEEE-mandated report count or structure. The reports share one fact base and differ only in emphasis. No report may claim access to confidential editor information or a final decision.

If the user asks for a real external peer review of a confidential submission, apply the venue's confidentiality and AI rules before processing. This suite is primarily for author-owned pre-submission review.
